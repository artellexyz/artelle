Buying

How buying works.

Works are sold as originals, as archival prints, and — for selected pieces — as digital editions. Email the studio to buy or to ask anything.

The original

One of one. Signed, with a certificate. Ships insured, worldwide.

The archival print

Pigment print on cotton rag, numbered and signed, in a fixed edition. Ships tracked, worldwide.

Selected works

The digital edition

A one-of-one digital edition of the work on a public ledger. Display it on any screen, prove ownership, resell it. Pay by card or ETH.

How it works

  1. Pick a work.

    Everything is in the catalogue.

  2. Email the studio.

    Say which piece and which format — original, print, or digital edition. We reply with price and availability.

  3. Delivery.

    Prints ship within days, tracked. Originals ship insured. Digital editions go to your wallet, or we hold them for you.
