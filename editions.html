Buying

Three ways to own
a work.

Every painting is offered one way or three ways. All of them are official; none of them require you to know anything technical.

The original

One of one. Signed on the back, wired and ready to hang. Ships insured with a certificate signed by the artist. The only version that can never be made again.

The archival print

Museum-grade pigment print on cotton rag, matched to the painting under the studio’s light. Each print is numbered, signed, and part of a fixed run. Ships flat or rolled, tracked, worldwide.

Selected works

The digital edition

The work’s official edition as a signed certificate on a public ledger. You can display it on any screen — a frame, a TV, a tablet on the wall — prove you own it anywhere, and resell it. Pay by card or ETH; we handle everything technical and can hold it for you.

How it works

  1. Choose the work.

    Browse the catalogue and open any piece to see what it’s offered as.

  2. Pick your edition.

    Original when it’s still available, print when you want the image for your wall, digital edition when you want the piece itself on your screens.

  3. We take it from there.

    Prints ship within days, tracked and insured. Digital editions are delivered to your wallet — or held safely for you until you want them.

Good to know

“Do I need a crypto wallet?” No. If you buy a digital edition by card, we hold it for you under your name until you ever want it moved — exactly like a gallery storing a work on your behalf.
